# Optional capstone: build your own

## Outcome

Build something you want to use. This is for advanced participants who finish
the required labs early; it is not required for workshop completion.

## Prerequisites

Finish the core labs. Choose a small goal you can demonstrate and verify.

## Participant steps

**Use:** Continue in this repo or open a fresh repository Session.

**Attach:** The relevant code, constraints, and your idea.

**Prompt:** Help me specify and build this use case using the workshop concepts.
Clarify the outcome in Interactive, show a Plan for review, then implement only
after approval. Suggest a concrete way to verify the result.

You could add a useful storefront feature, automate a repository task with an
agentic workflow, or build a small app or canvas. Your own idea is welcome.

**Expect:** A working result with a short explanation of what it does.

**Decide:** Review the Plan, permissions, and any cloud cost before execution.
Follow the repo's issue/Plan/task rules for material changes.

## Expected repository artifacts

Your outcome, reviewed Plan, changes, and verification evidence.

## Verification

Demo the result and explain how you know it works.

## Recovery

Narrow the goal to one useful behavior. No second repo, paid service, or new
framework is required.

## Stretch

Ask another participant to try it and record one improvement.
